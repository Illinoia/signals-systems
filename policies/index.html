Policies

Intro to Signals and Systems is a student-led course run through the Electronics & Engineering Club at Berkeley City College. It is based on UC Berkeley's EECS 16A and is not part of BCC's official course catalog.

Course Format

The course runs for 8 weeks, from Tuesday, October 27 to Friday, December 18, with 16 lectures in total.

Lectures

Lectures are held Tuesdays 1:30pm - 3:00pm and Fridays 12:00pm - 1:30pm in [Room TBA].

  • Lectures are hybrid. Coming in person is recommended, but joining on Zoom is fine: [Zoom link TBA].
  • Every lecture is recorded, and recordings are posted on the course homepage.

Prerequisites

None. The course is aimed at students heading toward electrical engineering, but it is open to everyone. Taking linear algebra at the same time is recommended but not required. You don't need linear algebra or differential equations beforehand.

Homework

There is no homework.

Attendance

Attendance is taken with a secret word system.

  • A secret word is announced during each lecture.
  • Enter the secret word in the attendance Google Form to be marked present.
  • If you miss a lecture, watch the recording and submit that lecture's secret word. It counts the same as attending live.

Certificate of Completion

The course is pass/no pass. Students who complete it receive a certificate of completion. There are two ways to earn it:

  • Attendance: attend at least 14 of the 16 lectures, in person, on Zoom, or by watching the recording.
  • Course project: if you can't meet the attendance requirement, complete a small course project instead. It takes about 3 hours. Details will be announced later.

These requirements may change. Any changes will be announced on the course homepage.
